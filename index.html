How it works

1

Say what you enjoy

The O*NET Interest Profiler, used by career counsellors worldwide, turns 60 likes and dislikes into six interest scores.

2

Rate where you are

Score yourself on the 12 skills and 10 knowledge areas O*NET uses to describe engineering work.

3

Compare and plan

See your match with each career, the gaps worth closing, and a starter roadmap you can tick off.

Built on real data, not guesses

Every career's interest profile, skill demands and knowledge demands come from O*NET, the U.S. Department of Labor's occupational database. Branch employability comes from the India Skills Report 2026.
