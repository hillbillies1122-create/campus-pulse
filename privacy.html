Privacy notice

Last updated 21 October 2026.

What we store

  • Your email address and a securely hashed password (handled by Supabase Auth).
  • What you enter: name, branch, year, CGPA, goal, subject marks.
  • Your interest profiler answers and scores, your skill ratings, your saved careers and your roadmap steps.
  • Your look settings (light/dark mode and background).

Who can see it

Only you. The database enforces row-level security: each signed-in user can read and change only their own rows. The project team does not look at individual records.

The Insights page shows averages across all users. A group is only shown when it has at least 5 people, and individual answers are never shown.

Where it is stored

In a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database hosted in the Mumbai (ap-south-1) region. The website itself is served by GitHub Pages. Fonts load from Google Fonts and the login library loads from jsDelivr.

Your controls

  • Download everything from Settings → Download my data.
  • Delete your account and all data from Settings → Delete my account. Deletion is immediate and permanent.
  • Edit any answer at any time.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking scripts, no selling or sharing data. Please don't enter sensitive personal information such as ID numbers.

Not professional advice

Campus Pulse is a student project for exploration. Its suggestions are not a professional career assessment. Talk to your faculty advisor or a career counsellor before big decisions.
